About CoffeeEQ

Your trusted partner for complete coffee equipment solutions.

Who We Are

CoffeeEQ is a professional coffee equipment sourcing and solution provider. We don't just sell machines — we help businesses build complete coffee operations.

With deep roots in the coffee industry, we understand not only the products, but the supply chain, the export process, and the real needs of our customers. Whether you're opening your first coffee shop or outfitting a 500-location chain, we bring the expertise and network to make it happen.

Our strength lies in three things: industry expertise — we know coffee equipment inside out; global sourcing — we've built a trusted network of over 100 verified manufacturers and suppliers; and export support — from quality inspection to logistics, we handle the complexities so you can focus on your business.

We are not a factory. We are your partner — the one who understands products, supply chain, and export, and who stands between you and risk.
