Frequently Asked Questions

What is the MOQ?

MOQ varies by product. For standard items, MOQ is 1 unit. For OEM/ODM projects, MOQ typically starts at 50–100 units.

What is the lead time?

Standard products: 7–15 business days. OEM/ODM projects: 30–60 days depending on complexity.

Do you offer OEM/ODM services?

Yes! We provide full OEM/ODM support including product design, branding, packaging, and specification customization.

What warranty do you offer?

12–24 months warranty depending on product category. Extended warranty options available.

What are the payment terms?

T/T, L/C, Western Union accepted. 30% deposit, 70% before shipment for standard orders.

What shipping methods do you offer?

Sea freight, air freight, and express shipping. We handle customs clearance and documentation.

Do you provide installation support?

Yes, we offer remote installation guidance and on-site support for large orders. Video tutorials and manuals included.

What after-sales service do you provide?

Lifetime technical support, spare parts supply, and maintenance guidance. Dedicated support team for each client.
